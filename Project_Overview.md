I'm excited to share that I've just built **TrustGrow** – a decentralized Web3 Escrow Marketplace designed specifically for freelance work. 

I built this project to solve a major problem in freelancing: trust and centralized fees. Instead of relying on a traditional platform, TrustGrow uses Ethereum smart contracts to act as a secure, automated middleman. 

Here are the main services it provides:
- **Secure Escrow Payments:** Client funds are locked safely on the blockchain. Freelancers know the money is secured, and clients know it won't be released until the work is approved.
- **Web3 Dashboard:** A fully built dashboard where you can manage your freelance orders, track progress, and get real-time blockchain updates.
- **On-Chain Dispute Resolution:** If any disagreements happen between a client and a freelancer, there is a built-in admin system to arbitrate and resolve things fairly on-chain.
